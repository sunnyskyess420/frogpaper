A guide for the prompt builder, right where the other tutorials live.

**Help**
- New **How to Build a Prompt** guide in Settings -> Help (and in the tutorial menu): eight steps covering how the rows become one sentence, what Subject and Setting really want, what Mode adds, the remaining rows, four rules, and a worked example with its fix.
- Written for this app: Color Palette, the rows on the left, the Avoid field. Same content as the mobile guide.
- The worked example is the useful part: Subject "Cozy" + Setting "cozy movie poster" + Cinematic gives the model a feeling and a format instead of a scene. The guide shows the row-by-row replacement, which moves "cozy" to Mood and names a thing and a place.

**Nothing else changed** - themes, gallery, providers and rotation are untouched.

**Install:** `FrogPaper-Setup-1.9.1.exe`
