FROGPAPER v1.3.0 - PINNED DROPDOWNS WITH STARS
==============================================

WHAT'S NEW (v1.3.0):
-------------------
✓ Theme-matching popup colors (auto-detects sv_ttk dark!)
✓ Pinned items move to "FAVORITES" section at top
✓ Mouse wheel scrolling works in dropdowns!
✓ Two sections: FAVORITES ★ and Available Options

HOW TO USE:
----------
1. Click any dropdown (Mood, Subject, Lighting, etc.)
2. Popup shows TWO sections:
   
   ★ FAVORITES (3)        ← Your pinned items
   ───────────────────
   ★ happy
   ★ calm
   ★ peaceful
   
   Available Options (71)
   ───────────────────
   ☆ angry              ← Click ☆ to pin!
   ☆ excited
   ☆ sleepy
   ...

3. Click ☆ star to pin item → moves to FAVORITES section
4. Click ★ star to unpin → moves back to available list
5. Click item text to select it

✓ Dropdowns stay readonly (not editable)
